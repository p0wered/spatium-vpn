# Privacy section concept set

Five browser-rendered vector studies for the section following Bypass. The set deliberately omits the future abstract WebGL accent and keeps the same product facts, typography and palette wherever the direction allows.

## Route / Record variants

1. `route-dual-plane`: text left, linked route and retention planes right.
2. `diagram-left`: technical cutaway left, explanatory text and evidence rail right.
3. `offset-spine`: wide route above an offset retention spine with a separate minimal-account record.

## Additional directions

4. `observer-ledger`: three-column visibility and retention ledger for ISP, Spatium and destination.
5. `ephemeral-infrastructure`: RAM-only server cutaway focused on the lifecycle of live session state.

## Review constraints

- Static desktop composition at `1440 × 1000`.
- Geist and Geist Mono from the production project.
- No shader, abstract light, final motion or production copy lock.
- Technical geometry is implementation-bound SVG rendered by the browser, not generated CGI.
- The selected concept receives a separate mobile composition after selection.
- Product statements such as RAM-only operation and account fields are design hypotheses until they are checked against the final Spatium architecture. They are not approved marketing claims yet.
